I Could Flip My World Cup Tickets for $1K Profit – Why I'm Holding

Jun 12, 2026
A friend made thousands flipping World Cup tickets, but one writer explains why he's not selling his despite a tempting $1,000 profit.

The Temptation of Easy Money

My friend made a small fortune flipping his World Cup tickets. He's now $2,000 richer, and I'm sitting on $300 lottery tickets that could net me a $1,000 profit right now. The math is simple: sell and cash in.

Why I'm Holding Firm

But here's the thing – some experiences are priceless. I didn't win the lottery to make a quick buck. I won it to witness history. And no amount of profit can replace the roar of the crowd or the thrill of the game.

The Lesson from a Friend's Success

My friend's windfall was a wake-up call, but it actually convinced me not to sell. Watching him cash out made me realize: I don't want to be the guy who looks back and regrets missing out on the moment. I'm holding on.
