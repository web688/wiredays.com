Graceland Buys ShedSync to Supercharge Shed Sales with AI and Automation!

Jun 12, 2026
Graceland Portable Buildings snaps up ShedSync to bring AI-powered inventory and marketing to portable building dealers. It's a digital revolution for the shed world!

Big News in the Shed World

Graceland Portable Buildings, the king of portable sheds, just dropped a bombshell: they're buying ShedSync, a tech startup that's all about making life easier for shed dealers. This isn't your grandpa's acquisition—it's a full-on digital makeover for the industry.

What's the Deal?

The two companies are joining forces to create a supercharged 'NextGen Dealer Platform.' Think automated inventory management that's smarter than a squirrel, AI-powered marketing that writes its own ads, and lead generation that'll have buyers knocking down your door. Plus, dealers get snazzy new websites that actually work.

The Big Picture

'This is about giving our dealers the same digital firepower the big boys have,' said a rep from Graceland. 'ShedSync's tech is like a secret weapon—automated, insightful, and built for the shed business.' The move cements Graceland's spot as the leader in portable building tech, and it's got competitors scrambling.
